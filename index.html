Optional: reflect on how your team works with AI

Keep a few working notes.

These six prompts record your view of the work. They don't measure maturity, certify a system or predict business results. Leave an item unanswered or choose Unknown when you need to ask the owner.

Answers stay in this page. Nothing is sent or saved automatically. Copy or download your notes if you want to keep them.

1. Your team mostly uses AI to look up information, rather than to help complete a defined piece of work.
2. Your team uses AI to draft output, but rarely asks it to examine options and trade-offs before a person decides.
3. Your team has no consistent checks for the content and structure of AI output.
4. One person is the only one who knows how to run or troubleshoot the workflow.
5. Your team is adding infrastructure before people use the basic workflow in their daily work.
6. Important decisions remain in chat, without an owned, searchable record that people can check later.
