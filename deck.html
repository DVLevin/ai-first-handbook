Before expanding the workflow

Make the working habits
part of the handover.

  • Review before acting

    Read generated files and source evidence. Structural validation cannot approve a business decision.

  • Keep the decision record

    Write the owner, change, reason and source. End sessions with completed work and the next owner.

  • Test another operator

    Have someone else use the guide, recognise a failure and run the fallback.

  • Expand on evidence

    Review the result and operating load. Sequence by readiness, dependencies and agreed capacity.
